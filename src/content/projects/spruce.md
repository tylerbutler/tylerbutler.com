Build terminal output with colors, boxes, tables, trees, semantic messages, and ANSI-aware alignment. Spruce uses tty to detect color support and adapts its output for terminals, pipes, and CI. It also supports syntax highlighting and Markdown rendering.

Spruce is logging-agnostic and runs on Erlang and JavaScript. Available under either the MIT or Apache-2.0 licence. The project uses Trellis for releases.
