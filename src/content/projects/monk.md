Find nearby players on radar and convert them to your faction through sustained proximity. Converted players stay in the game.

Monk is a prototype PWA with a Svelte interface, TypeScript networking, and Gleam game rules. The initial physical trial targets two iPhones; software checks do not establish GPS accuracy, Android behavior, or field feedback latency.
